# time-series-forecast
Time Series Forecasting is a statistical and machine learning technique used to predict future values based on historical data collected over time. It analyzes patterns such as trends, seasonality, and cyclic variations in past observations to estimate future outcomes. Time series forecasting is widely used in business, finance, weather prediction.

Key Components of Time Series Data:

Trend: Long-term upward or downward movement in data.
Seasonality: Regular and repeating patterns occurring at fixed intervals.
Cyclic Patterns: Long-term fluctuations without a fixed period.
Noise: Random variations that cannot be explained by patterns.

Common Time Series Forecasting Methods:

Moving Average
ARIMA (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average)
SARIMA (Seasonal ARIMA)
Exponential Smoothing
Prophet (Facebook Prophet)
LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory Neural Networks)
Transformer-based Models
Applications
Predicting future sales and revenue
Stock price forecasting
Weather and climate prediction
Energy consumption forecasting
Traffic and demand prediction
Healthcare monitoring

Example:

If a company has monthly sales data from 2020 to 2025, time series forecasting can be used to predict sales for upcoming months in 2026 based on historical patterns and trends.

Time Series Forecasting is the process of analyzing historical time-dependent data to identify patterns and predict future values. It helps organizations make informed decisions by estimating future trends, demand, and performance based on past observations.
